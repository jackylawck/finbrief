# Enterprise Risk, AI Governance & Legal Compliance (企業風險、AI 治理與法規合規)

## 1. Governing AI Deployment and Use (AI 部署與使用治理)
- **External Commercial AI Solutions**: Focus risk assessments on the deployment of third-party AI tools (e.g., Microsoft 365 Copilot) rather than internal proprietary ML model development. Evaluate vendor risk, data residency, and enterprise licensing terms (將風險評估重心放在第三方外部商業 AI 方案的部署，而非內部專屬模型開發。嚴格審查供應商風險、數據儲存地及企業授權條款).
- **ISO 42001 & AIMS Alignment**: Assess if the organization has an Artificial Intelligence Management System to continuously monitor algorithmic bias, data privacy, and security in deployed AI applications (評估企業是否具備 AI 管理體系，以持續監控已部署 AI 應用的算法偏見、數據隱私與安全性).

## 2. Legal & Common Law Compliance (法律與普通法合規)
- **IP Infringement & Liability**: Evaluate financial exposure to copyright infringement claims arising from generative AI outputs, and ensure appropriate contractual indemnifications are in place with AI vendors (評估因生成式 AI 輸出導致的知識產權侵權財務曝險，並確保與 AI 供應商有妥善的合約免責條款).
- **Data Protection Regulations**: Monitor provisions for GDPR or PDPO compliance, especially regarding cross-border data transfers and automated decision-making (監控跨境數據傳輸與自動化決策是否符合個資隱私法規).

## 3. Change Management & Human Capital (變革管理與人力資本)
- **Organizational Restructuring**: Analyze OPEX for costs related to cross-functional structural shifts driven by automation (分析因自動化驅動的跨部門結構調整所產生的營運開支).
- **Reskilling & Workforce Impact**: Evaluate investments in employee retraining and the financial impact of severance packages during transitional phases (評估過渡期間員工技能重塑的投資，以及潛在遣散費用的財務影響).
