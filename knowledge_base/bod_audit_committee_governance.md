# Board of Directors & Audit Committee Strategic Governance (董事會與審計委員會戰略管治框架)

## 1. Fiduciary Duties & AI Deployment Oversight (受託責任與 AI 部署監督)
- **Duty of Care, Skill, and Diligence (謹慎、技術與勤勉責任)**: Assess whether the board exercises independent judgment in approving third-party commercial AI deployments (e.g., enterprise Copilots). Directors must ensure they understand the vendor risks and do not merely rubber-stamp management's tech proposals (評估董事會在批准第三方商業 AI 部署時是否行使了獨立判斷。董事必須確保其了解供應商風險，而非僅僅對管理層的科技提案蓋章通過).
- **Risk Appetite & Strategic Alignment (風險胃納與戰略對齊)**: Evaluate if the capital allocation for AI tools and digital transformation strictly aligns with the Board's approved Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework and risk appetite, particularly regarding data privacy and copyright liabilities (評估投入於 AI 工具與數位轉型的資本配置，是否嚴格符合董事會批准的企業風險管理框架與風險胃納，特別是在數據隱私與版權責任方面).

## 2. Audit Committee & ISO 42001 Conformity (審計委員會與 ISO 42001 合規性)
- **Internal Audit & External Assurance (內部審計與外部鑒證)**: Scrutinize the Audit Committee's mandate in overseeing AI governance. Look for independent third-party audits or adherence to ISO 42001 (AI Management Systems) to verify that internal controls over deployed AI applications are effective and continually monitored (嚴格審查審計委員會在監督 AI 管治方面的職權。尋找獨立第三方審計或對 ISO 42001 人工智能管理體系的遵循，以驗證對已部署 AI 應用的內部控制是否有效且受到持續監控).
- **Materiality of Tech & Change Management Risks (科技與變革管理風險的重大性)**: Ensure the Audit Committee accurately identifies the financial materiality of failed change management initiatives or vendor lock-in. These risks must be properly disclosed in the Corporate Governance Report, not hidden within general OPEX (確保審計委員會準確識別變革管理失敗或供應商鎖定所帶來的重大財務影響。這些風險必須在企業管治報告中妥善披露，而非隱藏於一般營運開支中).

## 3. Shareholder Activism & Regulatory Disclosures (股東激進主義與監管披露)
- **HKEX/SFC Compliance & Transparency (港交所/證監會合規與透明度)**: Evaluate the robustness of disclosures in the Annual Report. The board must transparently communicate the ROI of AI investments and outline defensive strategies against data breaches to satisfy stringent regulatory requirements and protect minority shareholders (評估年報披露的健全度。董事會必須透明地溝通 AI 投資的預期回報，並概述防範數據外洩的防禦策略，以滿足嚴格的監管要求並保護小股東權益).
- **Defending Against Value Destruction (防範價值摧毀)**: Monitor signals that could attract activist investors, such as excessive and unmeasured spending on trending AI technologies without a clear path to profitability or operational leverage (監控可能吸引激進投資者的訊號，例如在缺乏清晰盈利路徑或營運槓桿的情況下，盲目且無法衡量地將資金揮霍於熱門 AI 科技上).
