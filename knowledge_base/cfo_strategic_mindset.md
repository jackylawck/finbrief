# CFO Strategic Capital Allocation & M&A Framework (CFO 資本配置與併購戰略框架)

## 1. Capital Allocation Efficiency (資本配置效率)
- **ROIC vs WACC**: Assess whether the Return on Invested Capital exceeds the Weighted Average Cost of Capital. Value is only created when ROIC > WACC (評估投入資本回報率是否大於加權平均資本成本，這是創造企業價值的唯一底線).
- **Free Cash Flow (FCF) Yield**: Evaluate the true cash-generating ability relative to enterprise valuation (評估相對於企業估值的真實現金創造能力).

## 2. M&A Due Diligence & Synergies (併購盡職調查與協同效應)
- **Goodwill & Intangible Traps**: Scrutinize historical acquisitions. Inflated goodwill often precedes massive impairment charges (審視過往併購，過度膨脹的商譽往往是巨額減值的前兆).
- **Synergy Realization**: Validate if projected revenue synergies or cost reductions (OPEX savings) are actually materializing post-merger (驗證併購後的營收協同效應或成本縮減是否真實兌現).

## 3. Shareholder Value & Dividend Policy (股東價值與派息政策)
- **Sustainable Payouts**: Ensure dividends and share buybacks are funded by structurally recurring FCF, not by taking on new debt (確保派息與股份回購由結構性自由現金流支撐，而非舉債).
