# CRO & CDO Data Governance and Risk Framework (首席風險官與數據官治理框架)

## 1. Data Governance & Data Drift (數據管治與數據漂移)
- **Data Quality & Poisoning Risks (數據質素與污染風險)**: Assess whether the organization has robust data cataloging and access controls before deploying enterprise generative AI (e.g., M365 Copilot). Poor internal data hygiene can lead AI to surface confidential or inaccurate financial data (評估企業在部署企業級生成式 AI 前，是否具備健全的數據目錄與存取控制。內部數據衛生不佳可能導致 AI 暴露機密或不準確的財務數據).
- **Data Drift & Concept Drift (數據漂移與概念漂移)**: Evaluate the operational risk of model outputs degrading over time as underlying business environments or input data structures change. Monitor costs associated with continuous auditing and recalibration of AI outputs (評估模型輸出隨著底層商業環境或輸入數據結構改變而退化的營運風險。監控與持續審計及重新校準 AI 輸出相關的成本).

## 2. Operational Resilience & Vendor Dependency (營運韌性與供應商依賴)
- **Third-Party Concentration Risk (第三方集中度風險)**: Analyze the systemic risk of relying exclusively on a single dominant AI vendor for critical business operations. Evaluate contingency plans, exit strategies, and potential vendor lock-in costs (OPEX) (分析企業核心業務過度依賴單一強勢 AI 供應商的系統性風險。評估應急計劃、退場機制以及潛在的供應商鎖定成本).
- **Incident Response & Business Continuity (事故響應與業務連續性)**: Check for provisions addressing AI-related business interruptions, such as API outages or critical algorithmic failures from external service providers, and their potential impact on revenue streams (檢查是否有針對 AI 相關業務中斷的應對措施，例如外部服務商的 API 停機或重大演算法故障，及其對現金流的潛在衝擊).

## 3. Algorithmic Accountability & Risk Frameworks (演算法問責與風險框架)
- **Alignment with Global Standards (與全球標準對齊)**: Scrutinize the organization's alignment with recognized AI risk management frameworks (e.g., ISO 42001 AIMS, NIST AI RMF) to demonstrate a structured approach to mapping, measuring, and managing AI deployment risks (審查企業是否與公認的 AI 風險管理框架對齊，以證明其具備結構化的方法來識別、衡量與管理 AI 部署風險).
- **Human-in-the-Loop [HITL] Governance (人機協作管治)**: Ensure that critical financial or HR decisions augmented by AI have mandatory human oversight mechanisms to prevent automated catastrophic errors (確保由 AI 輔助的關鍵財務或人力資源決策，皆具備強制性的人工監督機制，以防止自動化系統造成災難性錯誤).
