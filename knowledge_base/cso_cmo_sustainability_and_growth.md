# CSO & CMO Sustainability and Strategic Growth Framework (首席永續長與行銷/營收長增長框架)

## 1. Climate-Related Financial Disclosures & Green Finance (氣候相關財務披露與綠色金融)
- **IFRS S1/S2 & HKFRS Climate Standards (氣候披露準則與擱淺資產)**: Assess the integration of physical and transition climate risks into financial statements. Scrutinize whether legacy carbon-intensive assets are at risk of becoming "stranded assets" requiring accelerated depreciation or massive impairment charges (評估實體與轉型氣候風險如何整合至財務報表。嚴格審視高碳排的舊有資產是否面臨成為「擱淺資產」的風險，進而需要加速折舊或巨額減值).
- **Sustainability-Linked CAPEX & WACC (永續資本支出與資本成本)**: Evaluate capital expenditures (CAPEX) dedicated to the green transition. Check if the company is successfully leveraging Green Bonds or Sustainability-Linked Loans (SLL) to lower its Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) (評估投入於綠色轉型的資本支出。檢查企業是否成功利用綠色債券或可持續發展表現掛鉤貸款來降低其加權平均資本成本).

## 2. Revenue Engine, AI Commercialization & Customer Economics (營收引擎、AI 商業化與客戶經濟學)
- **Unit Economics: CAC vs. LTV (單位經濟學：獲客成本 vs 終身價值)**: Measure the efficiency of Sales & Marketing OPEX. Hyper-growth in revenue is financially toxic if the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) consistently outpaces the Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) (衡量銷售與行銷營運開支的效率。如果客戶獲取成本持續超越客戶終身價值，那麼營收的超高速增長在財務上是有毒的).
- **AI-Driven Top-Line Growth (AI 驅動的頂線增長)**: Analyze whether investments in customer-facing AI (e.g., hyper-personalization, dynamic pricing algorithms) are genuinely driving organic revenue growth and improving gross margins, or if they are merely expensive marketing gimmicks (分析對面向客戶的 AI 投資（如極致個人化、動態定價演算法）是否真正推動了有機營收增長並改善毛利率，抑或僅是昂貴的行銷噱頭).

## 3. Brand Equity & Reputational Risk (無形品牌價值與聲譽風險)
- **ESG Controversies & Goodwill Impairment (ESG 爭議與商譽減值)**: Monitor how ESG controversies (e.g., greenwashing, supply chain labor abuses) or data privacy scandals directly erode intangible brand equity, potentially triggering mandatory goodwill impairment tests under HKAS 36 (監控 ESG 爭議（如漂綠、供應鏈勞工剝削）或數據隱私醜聞如何直接侵蝕無形的品牌價值，並可能觸發 HKAS 36 下的強制性商譽減值測試).
- **Pricing Power Sustainability (定價權的持續性)**: Link the brand's sustainability narrative to its pricing power. Companies with strong, authentic ESG commitments often demonstrate resilient gross margins even during inflationary periods (將品牌的永續發展敘事與其定價權掛鉤。具備強大且真實 ESG 承諾的企業，往往能在通貨膨脹期間展現出具韌性的毛利率).
