# Core Financial Analysis Guidelines (IFRS/HKFRS)

1. **Balance Sheet (Static Snapshot)**
   - Core Equation: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity.
   - Focus: Assess liquidity by comparing current assets to current liabilities. Evaluate leverage (debt ratio). Negative equity is a critical red flag for insolvency.

2. **Income Statement (Dynamic Performance)**
   - Focus: Analyze Gross Profit Margin (pricing power and direct cost control) and Net Profit Margin (overall operational efficiency).
   - Red Flags: Declining revenue without a proportional decrease in OPEX indicates poor cost control.

3. **Cash Flow Statement (Actual Cash Movement)**
   - Core Concept: Profit does not equal cash.
   - Focus: Operating Cash Flow (OCF) must ideally be positive and growing. A profitable company with persistently negative OCF suggests poor earnings quality or receivable collection issues. Heavy reliance on Financing Cash Flow indicates debt dependency.
