# HKFRS & IFRS Financial Strategic Framework (香港財務報告與戰略分析準則)

## 1. Balance Sheet Robustness (資產負債表健全度)
- **Accounting Equation / 會計恆等式**: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity (資產 = 負債 + 股東權益)
- **Liquidity & Solvency / 流動性與償債能力**: 
  - Compare Current Assets against Current Liabilities (流動資產 vs 流動負債).
  - Watch for high leverage ratio or negative net equity (警惕高負債比率或資不抵債/負權益情況).
- **Asset Quality / 資產質素**: Check for Goodwill Impairment risks and inventory turnover deterioration (檢查商譽減值風險與存貨周轉惡化).

## 2. Income Statement Quality (損益表營運品質)
- **Gross Margin (毛利率)**: Reflects pricing power and direct cost efficiency (反映產品定價權與直接成本控制).
- **Net Margin (純利率)**: Reflects overall operational efficiency (反映整體經營效率).
- **Red Flags / 預警訊號**: Revenue decline accompanied by expanding OPEX (營業額下跌但營運開支不減反增).

## 3. Cash Flow Health (現金流健康度)
- **Operating Cash Flow (OCF / 經營活動現金流)**: Must be positive and support Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) (經營現金流必須持續為正，且足以覆蓋資本開支).
- **Earnings Quality / 盈利質素**: OCF / Net Profit ratio. If net profit is high but OCF is negative, watch for uncollected receivables or channel stuffing (若純利高但經營現金流為負，警惕應收帳款積壓或虛盈實虧).
