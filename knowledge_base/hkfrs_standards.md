# HKFRS & IFRS Financial Strategic Framework (香港財務報告與戰略分析準則)

## 1. Balance Sheet Robustness (資產負債表健全度)
- **Accounting Equation / 會計恆等式**: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity (資產 = 負債 + 股東權益).
- **Liquidity & Solvency / 流動性與償債能力**: 
  - Compare Current Assets against Current Liabilities (流動資產 vs 流動負債).
  - Watch for high leverage ratio or negative net equity / net assets (警惕高負債比率或資不抵債/負權益情況).
- **Asset Quality / 資產質素**: Check for Goodwill Impairment risks, bad debt provisions, and inventory turnover deterioration (檢查商譽減值風險、壞帳撥備與存貨周轉惡化).

## 2. Income Statement Quality (損益表營運品質)
- **Gross Margin (毛利率)**: Reflects pricing power and direct cost efficiency (反映產品定價權與直接成本控制).
- **Net Margin (純利率)**: Reflects overall operational efficiency (反映整體經營效率).
- **Red Flags / 預警訊號**: Revenue decline accompanied by expanding OPEX or aggressive revenue recognition (營業額下跌但營運開支不減反增，或激進的收入確認政策).

## 3. Cash Flow Health (現金流健康度)
- **Operating Cash Flow (OCF / 經營活動現金流)**: Must be positive and support Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) (經營現金流必須持續為正，且足以覆蓋資本開支).
- **Earnings Quality / 盈利質素**: Ratio of OCF to Net Profit. High net profit with negative OCF indicates uncollected receivables or channel stuffing (若純利高但經營現金流為負，警惕應收帳款積壓或虛盈實虧).

## 4. Capital Allocation & Shareholder Returns (資本配置與股東回報)
- **Return on Equity (ROE) & Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) / 股本回報率與投入資本回報率**: Crucial metrics for assessing management's capital efficiency (評估管理層資本運用效率的核心指標).
- **Dividend Policy & Share Buybacks / 派息政策與股份回購**: Evaluate if the payout ratio is sustainable and supported by Free Cash Flow (FCF) (評估派息比率是否可持續且有自由現金流支撐).

## 5. Third-Party AI Deployment & Governance (第三方 AI 部署與治理風險)
- **Vendor Risk & Commercial AI Solutions / 供應商風險與商業 AI 方案**: When analyzing tech spending, assess governance over external AI tools (e.g., enterprise Copilots). Evaluate procurement risks, vendor lock-in, and data residency rather than internal ML model development risks (在分析科技開支時，評估企業對外部 AI 工具的治理能力。著重審查採購風險、供應商鎖定及數據儲存地，而非內部模型開發風險).
- **ISO 42001 & AI Management Systems (AIMS) / 人工智能管理體系**: Look for signals of structured AI risk management, including impact assessments on data privacy, IP infringement, and algorithmic bias in deployed systems (尋找企業建立結構化 AI 風險管理的訊號，包含對已部署系統在數據隱私、知識產權侵權及算法偏見上的影響評估).
- **Shadow AI / 影子 AI**: Financial and security risks arising from unsanctioned employee use of generative AI (員工未經批准使用生成式 AI 所帶來的財務與資訊安全風險).

## 6. Legal, Compliance & Change Management (法律合規與變革管理)
- **Common Law & Regulatory Compliance / 普通法系與監管合規**: Exposure to litigation, intellectual property disputes, or cross-border data transfer fines (GDPR/PDPO) (面臨訴訟、知識產權爭議或跨境數據傳輸罰款的曝險).
- **Human Capital & Organizational Change / 人力資本與組織變革**: Assess restructuring costs, severance, and reskilling expenditures (OPEX) driven by AI automation or cross-functional structural changes (評估由 AI 自動化或跨部門結構調整所驅動的重組成本、遣散費及員工重塑技能開支).

## 7. Board of Directors & Fiduciary Oversight (董事會與受託責任監督)
- **Strategic Alignment & Risk Appetite / 戰略對齊與風險胃納**: Evaluate if the board effectively monitors strategic pivots and aligns tech/CAPEX spending with the company's risk appetite (評估董事會是否有效監督戰略轉型，並確保科技與資本開支符合公司的風險胃納).
- **Internal Controls & Audit / 內部控制與審計**: Pay attention to qualified audit opinions, auditor resignations, or material weaknesses in internal controls (高度關注保留審計意見、核數師辭任或內部控制的重大缺陷).
