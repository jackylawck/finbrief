# HKFRS & IFRS Financial Strategic Framework (香港財務報告與戰略分析準則)

## 1. Balance Sheet Robustness (資產負債表健全度)
- **Accounting Equation / 會計恆等式**: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity (資產 = 負債 + 股東權益).
- **Liquidity & Solvency / 流動性與償債能力**: 
  - Compare Current Assets against Current Liabilities (流動資產 vs 流動負債).
  - Watch for high leverage ratio or negative net equity / net assets (警惕高負債比率或資不抵債/負權益情況).
- **Asset Quality / 資產質素**: Check for Goodwill Impairment risks, bad debt provisions, and inventory turnover deterioration (檢查商譽減值風險、壞帳撥備與存貨周轉惡化).

## 2. Income Statement Quality (損益表營運品質)
- **Gross Margin (毛利率)**: Reflects pricing power and direct cost efficiency (反映產品定價權與直接成本控制).
- **Net Margin (純利率)**: Reflects overall operational efficiency (反映整體經營效率).
- **Red Flags / 預警訊號**: Revenue decline accompanied by expanding OPEX or aggressive revenue recognition (營業額下跌但營運開支不減反增，或激進的收入確認政策).

## 3. Cash Flow Health (現金流健康度)
- **Operating Cash Flow (OCF / 經營活動現金流)**: Must be positive and support Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) (經營現金流必須持續為正，且足以覆蓋資本開支).
- **Earnings Quality / 盈利質素**: Ratio of OCF to Net Profit. High net profit with negative OCF indicates uncollected receivables or channel stuffing (若純利高但經營現金流為負，警惕應收帳款積壓或虛盈實虧).
- **Financing Reliance / 融資依賴**: Excessive reliance on financing cash inflows to sustain operations signifies weak fundamental health (若主要靠融資活動現金流入維持營運，代表財務結構脆弱).

## 4. Capital Allocation & Shareholder Returns (資本配置與股東回報)
- **Return on Equity (ROE) & Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) / 股本回報率與投入資本回報率**: Crucial metrics for assessing management's capital efficiency (評估管理層資本運用效率的核心指標).
- **Dividend Policy & Share Buybacks / 派息政策與股份回購**: Evaluate if the payout ratio is sustainable and supported by Free Cash Flow (FCF), rather than funded by debt (評估派息比率是否可持續且有自由現金流支撐，而非舉債派息).
- **M&A and Expansion / 併購與擴張**: Assess whether recent acquisitions generate synergy or merely inflate intangible assets/goodwill (評估近期併購是否產生協同效應，還是僅僅膨脹了無形資產與商譽).

## 5. Tech, AI Investments & Intangible Assets (科技、AI投資與無形資產)
- **CAPEX vs OPEX / 資本開支與營運開支**: Under HKAS 38, distinguish whether tech infrastructure, AI deployments, and software developments are capitalized as Intangible Assets or expensed immediately (依據 HKAS 38，分辨企業的科技基建、AI 部署與軟件開發是資本化為無形資產，還是直接列為當期開支).
- **Impairment Testing / 減值測試**: Highly relevant for rapidly depreciating tech assets and legacy systems (對於快速折舊的科技資產與舊有系統，需高度關注減值測試結果).
- **Data Governance & Tech Risks / 數據管治與科技風險**: Financial impacts of data breaches, compliance fines, or obsolete tech stacks (數據外洩、合規罰款或技術棧老化對財務的潛在打擊).

## 6. Corporate Governance, Risk & Board Oversight (企業管治、風險與董事會監督)
- **Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) / 企業風險管理**: Evaluate board-level oversight on strategic, financial, and operational risks (評估董事會對戰略、財務及營運風險的監督力度).
- **ESG & Compliance / 企業社會責任與合規**: Regulatory compliance costs, carbon footprint financial impacts, and adherence to Listing Rules (合規成本、碳足跡的財務影響，以及對上市規則的遵循).
- **Internal Controls & Audit / 內部控制與審計**: Pay attention to qualified audit opinions, auditor resignations, or material weaknesses in internal controls (高度關注保留審計意見、核數師辭任或內部控制的重大缺陷).
